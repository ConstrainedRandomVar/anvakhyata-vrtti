1.1.50 is licensed where one sthānin could take several substitutes (a class such as guṇa, vṛddhi, yaṇ, jaś, car, bhaṣ, dīrgha) and the nearest must be chosen. It is not licensed in prohibitions, where the substitute is a single named sound, where the added element is an augment paired by 1.3.10, or where a commentator only mentions the sūtra (often with a wrong number: the Nyāsa labels उरण् रपरः as [[1.1.50]]).
