## 1. What 1.1.67 requires, from the sources

### C1

The word must be a 5th case used for the 5th-case meaning of direction ('from/after', dig-yoga with an unstated dik-word). Then the operation the sūtra prescribes is done to what FOLLOWS that word, never to what precedes it. Kāśikā and Nyāsa both say 1.1.67 is a restricting rule (niyama): it removes the doubt about which unstated dik-word (pūrva or para) to supply.

> तस्मादिति पञ्चम्यर्थनिर्देश उत्तरस्यैव कार्यं भवति, न पूर्वस्य।  
> — kashika 11067

### C3

The element after the 5th-case word must ALREADY BE THERE (sat), and the operation is done to it: substitution, augment, deletion/luk, accent, ṣatva/ṇatva, a kit/ṅit transfer, a saṃjñā, the choice of pada endings for an existing l. Placing a NEW suffix after a base (a suffix-introducing rule) is NOT 1.1.67's domain: the suffix's position comes from 3.1.2 परश्च. The Mahābhāṣya repeats the rule: 'in a suffix-introducing rule, 5th cases do not create a 6th (by 1.1.67)'.

### C4

Both a 7th and a 5th in one sūtra (ubhaya-nirdeśa): in general the 5th prevails (e.g. 6.1.186, 6.4.158, 7.1.90, 7.2.76, 7.2.83, 7.1.52, 7.3.112, 7.3.113, 8.3.32). But where the 7th has no other use (niravakāśa), it turns the 5th into a 6th and the operation falls on what PRECEDES. Examples: 7.2.82 आने मुक् (with अतः carried over), and 6.1.75–76 दीर्घात्/पदान्ताद्वा with छे (tuk goes to the long vowel, not to the छ).

### C5

The rule does not apply where the sūtra decides the direction itself, or where the 5th has a different job. (a) An explicit पूर्व ('before'), प्राक्, or आङ् ('up to'): the operation is then on what precedes, or within a range. 7.3.44: 'पूर्वस्य किम्? परस्य मा भूत्'. (b) A 5th that is not dig-yoga: cause (hetu), distance (अदूरात्), a 5th governed by an unstated अन्य ('other than', 8.3.4), or 'what the suffix was enjoined after' (विहितस्य). Nyāsa (3.1.2) treats a 5th as dig-yoga only when no other reason for the 5th exists. (c) A word that only looks like a 5th: a substitute written with t (तात्, आत् = the substitute आ), or a 6th that the Kāśikā keeps as a 6th.

> पूर्वस्येति किम्? परस्य मा भूत्  
> — kashika 73044

> पूर्वस्य किम्। सर्विकेत्यादौ टाबेकादेशं बाधित्वा कात्परस्य मा भूत् 'तस्मादित्युत्तरस्य' इति परिभाषणात्।

## 7. Open questions

1. **Suffix-introducing rules (the bulk: about 1,000 firings).** The sources are clear: placing a new suffix is 3.1.2 परश्च, not 1.1.67 (Mahābhāṣya on 3.1.2 and 4.1.60/5.1.23/5.2.49, Nyāsa and Padamañjarī on 3.1.2). The fix is probably to credit 3.1.2 for 'the suffix comes after it' and keep 1.1.67 for operations on a following element that is already there. The decision about how to re-label is yours.
2. **Commentator-'named' credits (layer 1).** Of the 32 layer-1 firings, 20 are not licensed and 2 unsure: the quoted passage where 1.1.67 is mentioned but rejected, discussed for another sūtra, or wrongly linked (3.2.1: a [[1.1.67]] tag on 1.1.68's words). Layer-1 credits need a check that the passage APPLIES the rule at this sūtra.
3. **The data's case codes.** 6.4.41 आत्, 7.3.1 आत्, 7.1.44 तात् are substitutes, not 5ths. G21/G24 (2nd/3rd case samartha words) and G8/G13 (6ths 'read as the 5th') come from the engine's own 'read as the 5th' step, for which no source was found.
