## 1. What 1.1.55 requires, from the sources

**C1** — What 1.1.55 says: a substitute (ādeśa) that has more than one sound (anekāl), or that is śit, replaces the WHOLE of the sthānin named in the 6th case. It is the apavāda of 1.1.52 अलोऽन्त्यस्य (single last sound). Scope = a substitution of a 6th-case sthānin; an augment, a new suffix, a nipātana, an atideśa, a prohibition, or a number/gender/case rule is outside it.

> अनेकाल् य आदेशः शिच् च, स सर्वस्य षष्ठीनिर्दिष्टस्य स्थाने भवति।  
> — kashika 11055

> <<अस्तेर्भूः>> [[२.४.५२]] — भविता।  
> — kashika 11055

> अलोऽन्त्यसूत्रापवादः  
> — kaumudi 11055

> न एकोऽनेकः, अनेकोऽल् यस्य सोऽनेकाल्, शकार इत् यस्य स शित्  
> — balamanorama 11055

> षष्ठीनिर्दिष्टस्य य उच्यत आदेशः, सोऽन्त्यस्यालः स्थाने वेदितव्यः।  
> — kashika 11052

**C2** — "More than one sound" is counted AFTER the anubandhas (it-letters) are removed: नानुबन्धकृतमनेकाल्त्वम्, made known by the separate mention of śit in 1.1.55. So तृ (= त्), उत् (= उ, the त् only marks tapara), अङ् (= अ), सिच् (= स्) are single-sound substitutes and replace only the last sound (1.1.52) unless the sthānin is itself one sound. The sources name 6.4.127 अर्वणस्तृ and 6.1.131 दिव उत् as places with NO sarvādeśa.

> भवत्येषा परिभाषा नानुबन्धकृतमनेकाल्त्वं भवतीति  
> — bhashya 11055

> शिद्ग्रहणमेवैतज्ज्ञापकम् । तेन <<अर्वणस्तृ>> [[6.4.127]] इत्यादेर्न सर्वादेशत्वम् ।  
> — paribhashendushekhar 6

> `नानुबन्धकृतमनेकाल्त्वम्`(व्या।प।14) इति ज्ञापनार्थम्। तस्य तु प्रयोजनम् -  <<दिव उत्>> [[6.1.131]]  इत्या-दिषु सर्वादेशाभावः॥

**C3** — Exception to C2 for an INITIAL it-letter that is an it only because the substitute is a pratyaya (चुटू 1.3.7, लशक्वतद्धिते 1.3.8, षः प्रत्ययस्य 1.3.6): when the substitute is enjoined it is not yet a pratyaya, so that letter still counts, the substitute is anekāl and replaces the whole; only then does the letter become an it.
