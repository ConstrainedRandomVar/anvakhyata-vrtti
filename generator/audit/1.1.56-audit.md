## 1. What 1.1.56 requires, from the sources

**C1** — What 1.1.56 is: an atideśa (extension). Because substitute and original are different, operations conditioned by the original would not reach the substitute; 1.1.56 makes the substitute behave like the original for those OPERATIONS (kārya), except operations conditioned by a single sound (al) of the original.

> स्थान्यादेशयोः पृथक्त्वात् स्थान्याश्रयं कार्यमादेशे न प्राप्नोतीत्ययमतिदेश आरभ्यते।  
> — kashika 11056

> स्थानिवदादेशो भवति स्थान्याश्रयेषु कार्येष्वनलाश्रयेषु, स्थान्यलाश्रयाणि कार्याणि वर्जयित्वा।  
> — kashika 11056

> आदेशः स्थानिवत्स्यात् न तु स्थान्यलाश्रयविधौ  
> — kaumudi 11056

**C2** — What counts as an ādeśa (and as a sthānin): the ādeśa is what, when enjoined, makes the other (that was due) cease; the sthānin is that in whose place something else is enjoined. An āgama (added element), a pratyaya newly introduced, a nipātana, a prakṛti-bhāva (no change), a vacana/liṅga/vibhakti rule, or an atideśa (-vat) introduces no substitute, so 1.1.56 has nothing to act on there.

> यस्य स्थानेऽन्यद्विधीयते तत्स्थानि । येन विधीयमानेन अन्यत्प्रसक्तं निवर्तते स आदेशः ।  
> — balamanorama 11056

**C3** — What is inherited: the status that operations of the śāstra depend on (dhātu-, aṅga-, kṛt-, taddhita-, avyaya-, sup-, tiṅ-, pada-hood …), so that a LATER rule conditioned by that status applies to the substitute. Only śāstric operations are extended.

> किमुदाहरणम्? धात्वङ्गकृत्तद्धिताव्ययसुप्तिङ्पदादेशाः।  
> — kashika 11056

> आर्धधातुके विषये प्रागेवादेशेषु कृतेषु <<धातोः>> [[३.१.९१]] इति तव्यादयो भवन्ति।  
> — kashika 11056

> इह हि स्थानिप्रयुक्तं यत् कार्यं शास्त्रीयं तदेवातिदिश्यते ।  
> — balamanorama 11056

> स्थानिवद्भावः शास्त्रीयेषु प्रवर्तते  
> — padamanjari 72061

**C4** — The anal-vidhi exception: no sthānivad-bhāva for an operation that depends on a sound (al) of the original (द्यौः, पन्थाः, सः).

> अनल्विधाविति किम्? द्युपथित्यदादेशा न स्थानिवद् भवन्ति।  
> — kashika 11056

> अलाश्रयकार्ये कर्तव्ये स्थानिवन्न भवतीति फलितम् ।  
> — balamanorama 11056

**C5** — 1.1.57–1.1.59 extend and limit it for vowel-substitutes (1.1.57: a vowel-substitute caused by what follows is sthānivat even in a rule about what precedes; 1.1.58: not in the listed rules; 1.1.59: for reduplication, before a vowel that conditions it). These three sūtras take आदेशः (and स्थानिवत्) from 1.1.56 by anuvṛtti.

> आदेशः स्थानिवद् इति वर्तते।  
> — kashika 11057

> अजादेशः परनिमित्तकः पूर्वविधौ कर्तव्ये स्थानिवद् भवति।  
> — kashika 11057

> पूर्वेणातिप्रसक्तः स्थानिवद्भाव एतेषु विधिषु प्रतिषिध्यते।  
> — kashika 11058

> द्विर्वचननिमित्तेऽचि अजादेशः स्थानिवद् भवति द्विर्वचन एव कर्तव्ये।  
> — kashika 11059

> स्थानिवदादेश॑ इतिअचः परस्मि॑न्निति चानुवर्तते ।  
> — balamanorama 11058

> 'स्थानिवदादेश' इत्यत आदेश इति  
> — balamanorama 11059

**C6** — Where commentators invoke it: at the point where a later operation is to be done ('… कर्तव्ये'), i.e. in deriving forms — not in stating the substitution rule itself. In an ādeśa-vidhi the vṛtti is 'Y-sthāne X ādeśo bhavati', which comes from 1.1.49 (6th case = sthāne) and 1.1.50–55 (which part is replaced); 1.1.56 adds nothing to that sentence. At ādeśa-vidhis commentators mention 1.1.56 either (a) in deriving the examples, (b) to explain the purpose of a word of the sūtra that cancels or replaces what sthānivad-bhāva would give (अपित्, ङित्, an anubandha), or (c) to say it does NOT apply. None of these changes the reading of the sūtra into its vṛtti. The reading genuinely depends on 1.1.56 only where its words are carried (1.1.57–59).

> स्थानिवद्भावेन कर्त्रभिप्रायक्रियाफलविवक्षायामात्मनेपदं भवति।  
> — kashika 24053

> स्थानिवद्भावात् पित्त्वं प्राप्तं प्रतिषिध्यते।  
> — kashika 34087

> नहि स्थानिवद्भावेन गाङिति रूपं लभ्यते॥  
> — kashika 24049

> अनल्विधौ [[१.१.५६]] स्थानिवद्भावः, अल्विधिश्चायम्।  
> — kashika 72011

**Test used.** A vṛtti-generator should credit 1.1.56 only where reading the sūtra into its vṛtti depends on it. That is true where the sūtra takes its words by anuvṛtti (1.1.57–59). It is not true in an ordinary substitution rule: there the vṛtti sentence comes from 1.1.49 and 1.1.50–55, and 1.1.56 matters only later in a derivation (C6).

## 4. Findings

That is a derivation-level credit, not a reading step. The project already hides it from the taught list (build.js ~l.2815), but it still shows in `P`.

4. **Mentions of purpose are not reading steps.** 1.2.5, 3.4.87, 3.4.103 (and 2.4.49, 2.4.54, 7.1.35, 7.1.36 in G1): the commentators use sthānivad-bhāva to explain why a word is in the sūtra (अपित्, ङित्, an anubandha). They could be explanatory notes, but not credits.
