## Main finding — the engine’s utsarga is wrong from 3.1.133 on (333 of 350 engine credits)

`build.js` `generalsFor()` (the block headed “general (utsarga) rules of the kṛtya section”) only knows three generals: तव्यत्/तव्य/अनीयर् (3.1.96), यत् (3.1.97), ण्यत् (3.1.124). It pairs every suffix vidhi in 3.1.91–3.4.117 with them by the dhātu’s final sound, ignoring the sense. But kṛtyas are taught only before ण्वुल् (Kāśikā 3.1.95) and only in bhāva/karman (3.4.70), and a suffix taught for another sense does not block them (Bhāṣya on 3.3.10: «भावे तुमुन्विधीयते कर्तरि ण्वुल्। तत्र कः प्रसङ्गो…»). So “क (3.2.4) / खश् / घञ् / णमुल् is an apavāda of तव्यत्/यत्/ण्यत्” is never right. Only the 17 credits inside the kṛtya section (3.1.97–3.1.126 vs तव्यत् etc.) are licensed — Bhāṣya on 3.1.97 «वासरूपेण तव्यदादयो भविष्यन्ति».

What the right entry would be for those 333 (on one primary credit per sūtra+suffix; the other 176 kṛtya-named copies are duplicates → none):
