## 1. What 1.1.46 requires, from the sources

C1. Content: an augment marked ṭ becomes the first part, one marked k the last part, of what is named in the 6th case. It places an augment (āgama) that a sūtra teaches.

> आदिष्टिद् भवति अन्तःकिद् भवति षष्ठीनिर्दिष्टस्य  
> — kashika 11046

> टित्प्रदेशाः — <<आर्धधातुकस्येड् वलादेः>>[[७.२.३५]] इत्येवमादयः  
> — kashika 11046

C2. Only augments: a ṭ/k marker on a suffix or on a substitute does not bring in 1.1.46 (a suffix cannot be part of anything; इट् in the tiṅ list is a substitute of ल).

> टित्करणं ङीबर्थम्; न तु <<आद्यन्तौ टकितौ>> [[1.1.46]]  इत्येवमर्थम्। कुत एतत्? आद्यन्तयोरवयवत्वात्। न च प्रत्ययोऽवयवो युज्यते  
> — nyaas 32008

> इटोऽप्यादेशत्वं तेन भवतीति न भवति देशवनिध्यर्थष्टकारः  
> — nyaas 34078

C3. The host: the item in the 6th case. With a 5th, the Kāśikā takes the following item (1.1.67) or, for तुक् after a long vowel, the preceding vowel.

> — kashika 64063

> दीर्घात् परो यश्छकारस्तस्मिन् पूर्वस्य तस्यैव दीर्घस्य तुगागमो भवति  
> — kashika 61075

C4. A sūtra that forbids the augment (also optionally: “वा … न भवति”) places nothing, so it does not need 1.1.46. Where the absence of इट् is given as a nipātana (7.2.64) the same holds.
