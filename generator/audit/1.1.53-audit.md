**C1 — 1.1.53 ङिच्च: a ṅit substitute (ādeśa), even one of several sounds, replaces only the LAST sound of the sthānin. It is the apavāda of 1.1.55 (sarvādeśa). Scope = a substitute that carries the anubandha ङ् and replaces a 6th-case sthānin of more than one sound.**

> ङिच्च य आदेशः, सोऽनेकालपि अलोऽन्त्यस्य स्थाने भवति।  
> — kashika 11053

> — kaumudi 11053

> — kaumudi 71093
