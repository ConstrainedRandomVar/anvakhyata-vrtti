## 1. What 1.1.66 requires, from the sources

### C1

Core rule: a word in the 7th case that stands for a following element (a para-saptamī, i.e. a sat-/aupaśleṣika-saptamī naming a sound, suffix or word) makes the operation fall on the element BEFORE it, never on the one after it. It is a restricting rule (niyama): the locative alone could point either way.

> तस्मिन्निति सप्तम्यर्थनिर्देशे पूर्वस्यैव कार्यं भवति, नोत्तरस्य।  
> — kashika 11066

> किं सत्सप्तमी ? किं वा गङ्गायां घोष इति वदौपश्लेषिकाधिकरणे सप्तमी ? इति ।  
> — padamanjari 11066

> तत्राऽचो वर्णान्तराधिकरणत्वं न संभवतीति सतिसप्तम्याश्रयणीया  
> — balamanorama 11066

> तस्मिन् तस्मात् इति पूर्वोत्तरयोर्योगयोरविशेषान्नियमार्थोऽयमारम्भः।  
> — bhashya 11066

### C2

Immediacy: 'nirdiṣṭa' means the operation falls on the IMMEDIATELY preceding element — no intervening sound (time-gaps outside saṃhitā are not counted). So a 7th that names something which follows a different, intervening element (e.g. परस्मैपदेषु after sic) does not by 1.1.66 make the earlier element the kāryin.

> निर्दिष्टग्रहणमानन्तर्यार्थम्। अग्निचिदत्रेति व्यवहितस्य मा भूत्॥  
> — kashika 11066

> सप्तमीनिर्देशेन विधीयमानं कार्यं वर्णान्तरेणाव्यवहितस्य पूर्वस्य बोध्यम्  
> — kaumudi 11066

> व्यवधानं चात्र वर्णकृतमेव निषिध्यते, नतु कालकृतम् ।  
> — tattvabodhini 11066

### C3

Only a 7th of a WORD-FORM, never of a meaning: a 7th naming a sense, a circumstance or a domain has no before/after relation and is outside 1.1.66.

> शब्दे सप्तम्या निर्दिष्टे पूर्वस्य कार्यं यथा स्यात्। अर्थे मा भूत्। जनपदे अतिशायने इति।  
> — bhashya 11066

> तु नेयं परिभाषा प्रवर्तते, सर्तम्यन्तातिशायनपदार्थस्य शब्दरूपत्वाभावेनाऽव्यवहितोच्चारितत्वरूपनिर्धिष्टत्वाऽसंभवात्  
> — balamanorama 11066

> शब्दे हि सप्तमीनिर्दिष्टे सतीयमुपतिष्ठते, नार्थे।  
> — nyaas 14110

> न ह्यर्थेन पौर्वापर्यमस्ति  
> — bhashya 64062

### C4

Kinds of 7th that are NOT 1.1.66 (the commentaries contrast them with the para-saptamī): (a) viṣaya-saptamī — 'when X is in view', the operation happening before X exists (e.g. the 2.4.35 adhikāra आर्धधातुके); (b) nimitta-saptamī of 2.3.37 (bhāva-lakṣaṇa), e.g. क्ङिति in 1.1.5; (c) a 7th naming an elision (luk/lopa), which cannot follow anything.

> विषयसप्तमी चेयं न परसप्तमी।  
> — kashika 24035

> आर्धधातुकशब्दस्यार्धधातुकत्वमनेकप्रत्ययसाधारणं सामान्यमर्थः, तेन च पौर्वापर्यं न सम्भवति।  
> — nyaas 24040

> निमित्तसप्तम्यां त्वस्यां सर्वत्र भवितव्यम्  
> — nyaas 11005

> तत्र तस्मिन् [[1.1.65]] इत्यादिकायाः परिभाषाया अनुपस्थानात्।  
> — nyaas 11005

> लुकीति विषयसप्तमी, दर्शनाऽभावस्य लुकः परत्वाऽसंभवात्  
> — balamanorama 73089

### C5

When a sūtra has both a 5th and a 7th, the 5th (1.1.67) prevails and turns the 7th into a 6th: the operation then falls on the 7th-named element itself (Mahābhāṣya examples: तास्यादिभ्यः लसार्वधातुके, गोतो णित्, रुदादिभ्यः सार्वधातुके, ईदासः, आमि सर्वनाम्नः सुट्, आण्नद्याः, याडापः, ङमो ह्रस्वादचि ङमुण्नित्यम्; PIS 71: डः सि धुट् by paratva).

> उभयनिर्देशे विप्रतिषेधात् पञ्चमीनिर्देशः।  
> — bhashya 11066

> उभयनिर्देशे पञ्चमीनिर्देशो बलीयान्  
> — paribhashendushekhar 71

> यत्र तु <<डः सि धुट्>> [[8.3.29]] इत्यादावुभयोरप्यचारितार्थ्यं तत्र <<तस्मिन्निति>> [[1.1.66]] सूत्रापेक्षया <<तस्मादित्युत्तरस्य>> [[1.1.67]] इत्यस्य परत्वात्तेनैव व्यवस्था।  
> — paribhashendushekhar 71

### C6

The 7th must name an element whose PRECEDING neighbour is the kāryin. Where the 7th-named item is itself the recipient — iṭ, which is always the ārdhadhātuka suffix's own āgama — 1.1.66 is not what decides (it would put the iṭ on the root).

> आर्धधातुकस्येट् यावानिण् नाम स सर्व आर्धधातुकस्य भवतीति विज्ञेयम्  
> — bhashya 64062

> यदि ह्रङ्गस्य स्यात्, `एकाच उपदेशेऽनुदात्तात्` [[7.2.10]] इति प्रत्ययस्येट्प्रतिषेधोऽनर्थकः स्यात्  
> — nyaas 72035

### C7

Scope: it applies even where there is no doubt (so an explicit 'पूर्व' in a sūtra does not rule it out); but by lakṣya it does not apply in कर्तृकर्मणोः कृति (2.3.65).

> एवं च महाविषयेयं भवति यदि सर्वत्र - सन्देहविषये, अन्यत्र च प्रवर्तते।  
> — nyaas 11066

> लक्ष्यानुरोधेन क्वचिदेवंजातीयकेष्वस्याः परिभाषाया अप्रवृत्तिरिति 'श्नान्नलोप' इति सूत्रे भाष्ये प्रपञ्चितत्वात्  
> — balamanorama 11066

### C8

Ekādeśa (6.1.84ff.): the one substitute replaces both neighbours by the sūtra's own पूर्वपरयोः; the Mahābhāṣya reads that as showing 1.1.66 and 1.1.67 do not both apply at once. So 1.1.66's 'operation on the preceding only' does not straightforwardly describe these rules.

> अचीत्येषा सप्तमी आदिति पञ्चम्याः षष्ठीं प्रकल्पयिष्यति तस्मिन्निति निर्दिष्टे पूर्वस्य [[1.1.68]] इति ॥  
> — bhashya 61084

> एवं तर्हि सिद्धे सति यत्पूर्वपरग्रहणं करोति तज्ज्ञापयत्याचार्यः - नोभे युगपत्प्रकल्पिके भवत इति ।  
> — bhashya 61084

## 5. Licensed — how

## 7. Open questions

1. **Ekādeśa (44 unsure).** Should the engine credit 1.1.66 for the 7th in ekādeśa rules (6.1.84–111)? Positionally the 7th is a para-saptamī, but the substitute replaces both neighbours by 6.1.84's own पूर्वपरयोः, and the Mahābhāṣya on 6.1.84 says the explicit पूर्वपरग्रहण shows 1.1.66/1.1.67 do not both apply. A distinct effect text ('fixes the two neighbours; replaced together by 6.1.84') would be accurate.
2. **iṭ rules (53 not licensed).** I judged every iṭ-āgama/iṭ-niṣedha 7th not licensed (the iṭ belongs to the suffix named in the 7th). Kāśikā still writes 'किति प्रत्यये परतः' in many of them. If you prefer to treat these as 'licensed via 7.2.35 + 1.1.46', say so — the call rests on Nyāsa 7.2.35 and the Mahābhāṣya on 6.4.62, not on a statement in the commentaries on 1.1.66 itself.
3. **Qualifier 7ths (QUAL/QUAL72, 22).** परस्मैपदेषु after सिच्, सन्-चङोः after णि, चङ्परे etc. I marked them not licensed because of the adjacency condition (C2). They are clearly nimittas of the rule, just not adjacent to the kāryin — the vṛtti wording ('X परे') can stay; only the 1.1.66 credit is wrong.
4. **Nipātana (27 unsure).** No source analyses these 7ths step by step.
5. **Generic ārdhadhātuka.** Kāśikā declares the 2.4.35 adhikāra a viṣaya-saptamī (22 not licensed), but says nothing for 6.4.46; there I followed Nyāsa's 'particular ārdhadhātuka ⇒ para-saptamī' and counted 8 firings licensed. Bālamanoramā/Padamañjarī also make individual 7ths viṣaya in 6.4.43 ये, 7.1.89 सर्वनामस्थाने, 7.3.17 (left unsure).
6. **Meaning words naming the uttarapada (WORDCLASS, 11 licensed)** — e.g. शिल्पिनि, गुणवचनेषु, पूगेषु. I counted these licensed when the meaning word is the only word naming the following uttarapada (Mahābhāṣya on 6.4.62 lets a meaning-7th stand for the word that expresses it). When a separate form-word names the following element and the meaning word only adds a semantic condition (प्रमाणे, उपमाने, कर्मणि, अनपत्ये …) I counted it not licensed. This line is mine, not a commentator's.
