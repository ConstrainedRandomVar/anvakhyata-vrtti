Beta. The vṛttis, their steps and the paribhāṣā credits are machine-built, with AI help, and are still being reviewed — there may be mistakes. Check them against the Kāśikā and the Kaumudīs shown beside each sūtra, and if something is missing or wrong, tell us with ⚑ report on that sūtra.
